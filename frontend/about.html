About IdeaBoard

IdeaBoard is the suggestion space for ABC College. Students, parents, and staff post ideas, follow their status, and see how the college responds.

One college

Every suggestion belongs to ABC College. There is no public internet board and no other campus.

Open status

A suggestion moves from Open to Reviewing, On the agenda, In progress, Done, or Rejected. The status is visible to the college community.

Community votes

Logged-in members can upvote a suggestion or a comment. Votes help staff see what has the most interest.

Staff response

Staff and admins review the board, update status, and manage access. Admins can change who is staff or admin.

Who uses it

  • Members post suggestions, comment, and vote.
  • Staff review the board and update suggestion status.
  • Admins do everything staff can, and manage people’s access.
