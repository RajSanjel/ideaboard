Before you post

Suggestion guidelines

A useful suggestion is specific, about college life, and something the college can actually act on.

What to include

  • A clear title. Say the change, not just the topic.
  • Who it helps: students, parents, a department, or the campus.
  • What is wrong now, and what you want instead.
  • The right category, such as Facilities, Cafeteria, or Transport.

What not to post

  • Personal complaints about a named student or staff member.
  • Duplicate ideas. Search the board and upvote the existing one.
  • Requests that are outside the college, or private academic records.
  • Spam, ads, or content that is not for the college community.

After you post

  • You can edit or delete your suggestion for 15 minutes.
  • Comments and replies follow the same 15-minute window.
  • You cannot upvote your own comment.
  • Status changes are made by staff. Voting does not change status.

Status meanings

  • Open: submitted, waiting for a response.
  • Reviewing: staff are looking at it.
  • On the agenda: scheduled for a decision.
  • In progress: work has started.
  • Done: completed.
  • Rejected: will not be taken forward.
